01 — The Hardware

The Pill Dispenser

A round base with 10 compartments. An orange servo arm rotates to the right compartment and pushes one pill down the chute. An IR sensor confirms the pill fell. That's it.

The actual working prototype
01

Red Base Disc

10 compartments — one for each medicine. Fixed, never moves.

02

Orange Servo Arm

Rotates to the right compartment at the scheduled time and pushes the pill down the chute.

03

IR Sensor

Detects when the pill actually falls (~20ms response). Stops the servo immediately.

04

Arduino + 18650 Batteries

Brain of the system. Dedicated battery power for servos — prevents resets and jitter.

Close-up: servo arm + compartments
ESP32 Panic Remote — 4 buttons

The Panic Remote

A wireless ESP32 device with 4 tactile buttons. Patient keeps it with them. Press a button — an alert immediately fires on the caretaker's phone via Telegram.

02 — Caretaker Interface

CareDispense Control App

The caretaker opens this app on their PC. They set which medicine goes in which channel, when it should dispense, and how many pills are left. The dispenser does the rest automatically.

CareDispense — Pill Dispenser Control
💊

Per-Channel Schedule

Each channel has its own medicine name, stock count, and time slots — Morning, Afternoon, and Evening. Caretaker sets it once, runs every day.

⚡

Dispense Now Button

Caretaker can trigger any channel manually at any time — for testing or emergency manual dose.

📦

Stock Tracking

Shows how many pills remain in each compartment. One click to add refills.

📡

Telegram Integration

"Test Telegram" button sends a live test alert to the caretaker's phone — confirms the alert pipeline is working.

03 — Patient App

CareSaathi

A separate app designed for the patient — warm, simple, large buttons. Music, meditation, companionship, and emergency SOS. All in one place.

🏠

Home

Pick language, auto-curated music playlist (Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu + more)

💬

Companion — Meera

A warm AI friend that chats in the patient's language. Has a box to message caretaker directly on Telegram.

🧘

Meditate

Caretaker picks a track + time. Auto-plays daily. Good for morning prayers, relaxation.

🚌

Traveler Mode

Queue 10–20 songs for a long journey, auto-stop timer so music doesn't play all night.

🆘

SOS — Gesture Emergency

Camera monitors for a raised hand in background. Hold hand up for ~2 seconds → 5-second cancel countdown → Emergency Telegram alert sent with video clip. Hands-free emergency call.

⚙️

Settings

Groq API key + Telegram bot token stored locally. Caretaker configures once — patient never touches settings.

05 — Emergency Alerts

What Happens in an Emergency

Any alert — from panic button, gesture SOS, missed dose, or companion chat — goes straight to the caretaker's Telegram. They can also request a live photo or video from the patient's room.

4 Types of Alerts

🚨 EMERGENCY

"EMERGENCY — PATIENT IN EXTREME DANGER. Ambulance needed immediately."

🙋 Help Needed

"Patient is not in danger but would like some assistance when you get a chance."

💊 Dispense Error

"Something went wrong while dispensing. Please come check on the patient and device."

✅ I'm Okay

"Patient pressed the check-in button — no help needed right now."

🎥 Human Verification — "Is the patient really okay?"

Caretaker simply sends a message to the Telegram bot. The PC at home responds instantly.
