01 — Core Hardware Prototype

Smart 10-Channel Round Dispenser

Designed for ultra-low cost, high mechanical accuracy, and zero missed doses. The hardware operates on dual power sources to prevent micro-controller reset during servo load.

Live Physical Model
Physical Prototype — 10-slot rotating base disc, high-torque micro servo arm, IR fall sensor, and custom Arduino controller unit.
01

10-Compartment Base Disc

Fixed outer ring holding individual pill doses securely separated. Non-jamming slot design.

02

Precision Servo Arm

Rotates to the scheduled channel and nudges the pill cleanly into the discharge chute.

03

IR Drop Sensor Beam

Positioned at the chute outlet. Detects pill passage in ~20ms to confirm successful delivery.

04

Dual Battery Power Shield

Independent 18650 power for servos prevents electrical noise and voltage dips on the logic board.

Wireless Safety Accessory

ESP32 Handheld Panic Remote

Completely mobile 4-button tactile remote for the elderly patient.

🚨

SOS Emergency

Triggers loudest alert to caretaker phone instantly with GPS/Room tag.

✅

I'm Okay Check-in

Cancels active alerts or notifies caretaker that dose was taken safely.

🙋

Assistance Needed

Non-urgent notification for assistance with water, food, or mobility.

💊

Dispense Error

Signals if a pill didn't fall or got stuck for immediate caretaker check.

03 — Patient Experience

CareSaathi Mobile & Web Companion

Designed specifically for senior citizens with large touch targets, warm conversational AI, devotional music, and zero-touch emergency triggers.

🌐

Multi-Lingual Audio

Supports Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Punjabi, and English. Curated music and audio suggestions for emotional wellness.

💬

Meera AI Companion

Warm, friendly voice/text companion powered by Groq LLM. Allows the patient to type notes sent straight to caretaker's Telegram.

🧘

Scheduled Meditation

Caretaker can set daily morning prayers or meditation chants (e.g., Ram Chant at 10:30 AM) that auto-play to calm the patient.

🚌

Traveler Mode

Queue 10–20 songs for long car journeys with an auto-stop sleep timer so battery isn't wasted.

📷

MediaPipe Gesture SOS

Webcam runs MediaPipe hand tracking. Raised hand held for ~2 seconds initiates SOS with a 5s cancellation timer before alerting caretaker.

⚙️

Zero-Touch Settings

API keys and bot configurations are managed on the caretaker end. Patient gets a clean, non-confusing UI.
